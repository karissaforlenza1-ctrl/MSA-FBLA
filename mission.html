Built from the ground up

Our mission.

This is the first year of FBLA at Manatee School for the Arts, and we are proud to be building our chapter from the ground up. Our mission is to develop confident student leaders who are creative, service-driven, and ready to make an impact in business, community, and beyond.

What we stand for

Our chapter helps students grow through leadership, professionalism, service, and entrepreneurship. We are a new chapter, but we bring ambition, determination, and a commitment to excellence. We do not compete just to try; we compete to win. We represent Manatee School for the Arts with pride while creating opportunities for students to learn, serve, connect with the business community, and succeed in competition.

“We dont compete just to compete; we compete to win!”
