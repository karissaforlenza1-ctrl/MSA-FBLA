Why FBLA through MSA?

Through MSA FBLA, students can discover their strengths, build confidence, and prepare for the future while staying connected to the creativity of Manatee School for the Arts. You will gain real-world business skills, meet new people, serve your community, and find opportunities to compete, lead, and grow.
